## Methods seen (for chapter text)

Specim IQ hyperspectral (VNIR), SWIR fusion, white correction (Spectralon / ColorChecker), Lambda 1050 spectrophotometer 250–2500 nm, linear unmixing ochre/stone, PCA, hierarchical clustering (dendrogram), SVM, decorrelation stretch, GigaPan, Matterport 3D, Gaussian splatting, LabelMe + ML segmentation, neural nets for faded figures, VR water-level reconstruction (−18 m).
